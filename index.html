AD Consulting Bookkeeper

A QuickBooks Online integration operated by A.D. Consulting, Sacramento, California.

AD Consulting Bookkeeper is the tool A.D. Consulting's bookkeeper uses to read a client's QuickBooks Online company and, with that client's approval, record and correct entries, reconcile accounts and prepare reports under US GAAP. It is an internal tool for A.D. Consulting's bookkeeping engagements. It is not sold, offered on the Intuit App Store, or run on anyone else's behalf.

A client connects a QuickBooks Online company by signing in with Intuit and granting the app accounting access. The client can revoke that access at any time from inside QuickBooks Online or by asking us.

Documents

  • End-User License Agreement, the terms under which a client lets A.D. Consulting connect the app to its QuickBooks Online company.
  • Privacy Policy, what QuickBooks data the app reaches, how it is used, stored, shared and deleted.
  • Connect or reconnect, how a connection is set up and renewed.
  • Disconnect, how to revoke the app's access and what happens to your data afterwards.
