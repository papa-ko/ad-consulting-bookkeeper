Connect or reconnect QuickBooks Online

For clients of A.D. Consulting.

Connections are set up together with your bookkeeper during a scheduled session. There is no self-service sign-up. If you have landed here on your own, email andrew.j.courtright@gmail.com and we will arrange a time.

What happens when you connect

  1. Your bookkeeper opens an Intuit authorization link for AD Consulting Bookkeeper.
  2. You sign in to Intuit yourself. A.D. Consulting never asks for, sees or stores your Intuit password.
  3. Intuit shows the company and the permission requested: accounting data (read and write). You choose which company to connect and click Connect.
  4. Intuit returns an access token to the app. From then on the app reads and writes your books through Intuit's API under that token. Access tokens expire after one hour and are renewed automatically for up to 100 days of inactivity.

Reconnecting

If a connection expires, is revoked, or your company is moved, your bookkeeper will ask you to repeat the steps above. Nothing in your books is changed by connecting or reconnecting; the connection only grants access.

Before you connect

Please read the End-User License Agreement and Privacy Policy. Connecting a company means you agree to them. You can disconnect at any time.
