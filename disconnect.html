Disconnect AD Consulting Bookkeeper

You can revoke the app's access to your QuickBooks Online company at any time.

Option 1: from inside QuickBooks Online

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as a company admin.
  2. Open Apps in the left menu, then My apps.
  3. Find AD Consulting Bookkeeper, open its menu and choose Disconnect.

Intuit revokes the app's access immediately; no further reads or writes are possible. Our weekly connection check then sees the revoked connection and deletes the stored tokens for your company.

Option 2: ask us

Email andrew.j.courtright@gmail.com with the company name. We call Intuit's token revocation endpoint from our side, delete the stored tokens the same business day, and confirm by email.

What happens to your data

  • Your QuickBooks Online company is unchanged. Disconnecting removes our access; it does not delete or alter any entry in your books.
  • The stored access and refresh tokens for your company are deleted.
  • Working papers already prepared for your engagement (reconciliations, reports, supporting schedules) are kept for the period stated in the Privacy Policy and can be returned to you or deleted earlier on request.

You can reconnect later if you resume services.
