Privacy Policy

AD Consulting Bookkeeper. Effective October 19, 2026. Last updated October 20, 2026.

This policy explains what data the AD Consulting Bookkeeper application (the "App") reaches when a client connects a QuickBooks Online company, and how A.D. Consulting uses, stores, shares and deletes that data. It is written in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, email us at the address at the bottom.

1. Who we are

A.D. Consulting is a sole proprietorship owned by Andrew Duarte, based in Sacramento, California. We provide bookkeeping services to small businesses. The App is our internal tool for working in clients' QuickBooks Online companies. It is used only by our bookkeeper and by software assistants under the bookkeeper's direct control. A.D. Consulting is the controller of the data described here.

2. What the App accesses

When you connect, Intuit asks you to grant the App the accounting permission (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting). With it the App can read and, where we are engaged to, write the accounting records in the company you connect, such as:

  • company information: legal name, address, fiscal year, QuickBooks company ID;
  • chart of accounts, account balances and classes;
  • transactions: bank and credit card feeds, deposits, expenses, bills, invoices, payments, transfers and journal entries, including their dates, amounts, memos and attachments' metadata;
  • customer and vendor lists, including names, billing addresses, email addresses and phone numbers you have stored in QuickBooks;
  • reports, such as the trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet and general ledger.

The App does not request payroll, payments-processing or Intuit identity (OpenID) permissions, and it never receives your Intuit password. You sign in to Intuit yourself.

Besides QuickBooks data, the App stores the OAuth tokens Intuit issues for your company (an access token that expires after one hour and a refresh token that expires after 100 days without use) and your QuickBooks company ID. Our own records of the work (notes, reconciliations, change logs) are kept with your engagement file.

3. How we use it

We use your accounting data only to deliver the bookkeeping services you engaged us for: categorizing and recording transactions, reconciling bank and credit card accounts, preparing financial statements and schedules, cleaning up prior-period errors, and answering your questions about your books. We use the connection tokens only to keep that access working.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build profiles of you or your customers for any other purpose, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Our legal basis and your consent

We process your data to perform the engagement contract between you and A.D. Consulting and because you consented when you connected the company through Intuit. You can withdraw that consent at any time by disconnecting. Where we keep records after the engagement, we do so to meet our professional and legal record-keeping obligations and to defend our work if it is questioned.

5. Software assistants

Our bookkeeper uses AI software assistants to speed up routine work such as reading statements, suggesting categories, drafting reconciliations and preparing reports. When used on your engagement, those assistants may process your accounting data. They act only on the bookkeeper's instructions, every change to your books is reviewed by a person before it is posted. Your accounting data goes only to an assistant whose terms limit the use of that data to serving us: today that is Anthropic's Claude, with model-training use of our conversations kept switched off in the account settings; before we connect client companies in production we are moving that work under Anthropic's commercial terms, which prohibit training on customer content and include a data processing agreement. Google's Gemini assistant is used only for research and for drafting templates and never receives your accounting data. The providers we use are listed in the next section.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid subpoena, and we will tell you first unless the law forbids it. If A.D. Consulting is ever sold or merged, your data would pass to the successor under this same policy and you would be told in advance.

7. Where it is stored and how we protect it

Your data is stored in the United States on A.D. Consulting's own computers, which use full-disk encryption and are protected by passwords and automatic locking, and in the Google Workspace account used for the business, which is protected by two-step verification. Connection tokens are kept in a file readable only by the bookkeeper's user account; they are never written into documents, emails or chat. The App talks to Intuit only over HTTPS. Access to client data is limited to the bookkeeper. We review access and rotate tokens when a device is replaced or a person leaves.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data we will tell you promptly, and within the time California law requires, with what we know and what we are doing about it.

8. How long we keep it

  • Connection tokens: deleted when you disconnect, when the engagement ends, or when Intuit expires them, whichever comes first.
  • Working papers (reconciliations, schedules, reports, change logs, copies of statements you gave us): kept for the engagement plus seven years, which matches the record-retention period commonly expected for bookkeeping work supporting tax filings. You may ask us to return and delete them earlier; we will do so unless a law or an open dispute requires us to keep them.
  • Your books themselves live in your QuickBooks Online company, which you control. We do not keep a separate copy of the full company file.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect any time. In QuickBooks Online go to Apps, My apps, AD Consulting Bookkeeper, Disconnect. Or email us. See the Disconnect page.
  • Access and correction. Ask us what data about your company we hold outside QuickBooks and we will tell you and correct anything that is wrong.
  • Deletion. Ask us to delete working papers early, subject to the retention exceptions above.
  • Portability. Ask for your working papers in a standard format such as PDF, CSV or Excel.

Send requests to the email address below. We verify that a request comes from the business owner or an authorized administrator before acting, and we respond within 30 days.

10. California residents

A.D. Consulting is a small business and does not meet the revenue or data-volume thresholds that make the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) apply to it. We nevertheless honor the requests described in section 9 for everyone. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

11. Children

The App is a business tool. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and the App is not directed at children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always at this address with its effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your data we will email you before it takes effect.
